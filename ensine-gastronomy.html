A new product needed more than a launch campaign.

EnsinE was preparing to launch a new online degree program in Gastronomy. The challenge was to define its positioning and build a content structure that connected audience interests and search demand with what the program offered.

My role

I developed the program's product marketing strategy with the CMO.

I designed the information architecture for EnsinE's program pages and an organic content system built around topic clusters and content funnels, connecting search visibility, in-depth editorial content and lead-generation assets.

I led EnsinE's broader organic content strategy and wrote the copy for the Gastronomy program page.
