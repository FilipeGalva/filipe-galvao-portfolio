About

Journalism — UFF · Master's in Communication and Culture — UFRJ Rio de Janeiro · Remote

I'm Filipe Galvão, a Content Strategist & Journalist based in Rio de Janeiro. A journalist by training, with a master's in Communication and Culture, I moved from reporting and academic research into content strategy, brand positioning and audience development. The common thread running through my work is sensemaking: bringing information from multiple sources together to guide decisions.

I start by investigating the problem, checking sources and understanding the audience. I use those findings to guide positioning, editorial decisions, each channel's role and performance measurement. My aim is to make complex knowledge clear and useful, with a coherent strategy behind what gets published.

I'm also the founder of NOSMUS, an independent venture applying research and systems thinking to organizational intelligence and knowledge work.
